1. The Lighthouse (mystery)

1. You arrive at an abandoned lighthouse at dusk. The door hangs open and a light flickers above.

Climb the stairs → 2
Check the cellar → 3

2. At the top, a lantern burns beside a note: “Don’t trust the keeper.” Footsteps echo behind you.

Hide behind the lens → 4
Confront whoever is coming → 5

3. The cellar is full of crates stamped with a ship’s name. A hatch in the floor leads to a sea cave.

Open the hatch → 6
Examine the crates → 7

4. A figure in a raincoat snuffs the lantern, then signals to a boat offshore.

Follow the figure down the cliff path → 8
Signal the boat yourself → 9

5. The keeper appears. “You’re the third detective this year,” he says, holding out a kettle.

Accept his tea → 10
Demand the truth → 11

6. The sea cave floods as the tide rises. A rowboat is tied nearby, and a rocky ledge climbs the wall.

Take the rowboat → 12
Climb the ledge → 13

7. The crates hold maps and a thick ledger.

Take the ledger → 14
Burn the evidence → 15

8. The figure hurries along the cliff edge, and you gain on them.

Call out → 16
Leap to tackle them → 17

9. The boat swings toward shore, its lamp blinking a code.

Flash the code back → 18
Flash a random pattern → 19

10. The tea is warm and strangely sweet.

Drink it all → 20
Pour it out when he’s not looking → 21

11. The keeper sighs and looks at the sea.

Press him harder → 22
Soften your tone → 23

12. THE END: Daring Escape. You row to the mainland and hand the coast guard the smugglers’ location.

13. THE END: Wrong Rescuers. Fishermen pull you from the ledge at dawn. They’re the smugglers.

14. THE END: Headline Detective. The ledger names the mayor, and you make the front page.

15. THE END: Beacon of Flame. The fire climbs the tower and lights the coast for miles. Everyone comes to see.

16. THE END: Family Secrets. The figure is the keeper’s daughter, who confesses it all to you.

17. THE END: A Long Swim. You both tumble into the sea, and you wash up in the next town with a great story.

18. THE END: Smuggler’s Share. The captain welcomes you aboard and offers a cut. You take it.

19. THE END: Cold Case. The boat flees and never returns, and the case goes cold.

20. THE END: The Vanishing Lighthouse. You wake on the dock, and the lighthouse is gone from the map.

21. THE END: A Ghost’s Company. You discover the keeper has been dead for decades. You stay for the stories.

22. THE END: Treasure Hunters. He confesses he’s guarding buried treasure, and you split it.

23. THE END: The Lonely Keeper. He wrote the notes himself because he wanted visitors. You visit every month.
